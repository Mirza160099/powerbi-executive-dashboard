# KPI Architecture

## Level 1 — Executive Outcome KPIs

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Revenue vs Target
- YoY Growth

These answer whether the business is delivering the desired result.

## Level 2 — Performance Drivers

- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Region
- Channel Performance
- Segment Performance
- Average Order Value
- Discount Rate

These explain *why* top-level outcomes moved.

## Level 3 — Diagnostic Detail

- Orders
- Units
- Discount
- Product/channel mix
- Drillthrough transactions

These help analysts investigate specific performance issues.

## Principle

Executives should see **signal → driver → drillthrough**, not a wall of metrics.
