# Executive Storytelling

A strong dashboard should answer a sequence of questions:

## 1. What happened?
Revenue is above/below target.

## 2. Where?
Specific regions/products/channels explain the variance.

## 3. Why?
Mix, volume, discount or margin changes contribute.

## 4. What should we investigate?
Drill through to underperforming areas.

## 5. What action could follow?
Pricing review, sales focus, channel optimisation, cost/margin review, or forecast adjustment.

The dashboard should support decisions rather than simply display charts.
